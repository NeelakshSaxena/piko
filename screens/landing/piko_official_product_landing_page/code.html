Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

Clear, honest answers about Piko, hosting differences, and platform support.

What is Piko?

Piko is a modern personal security and family safety platform that lets parents and children stay connected through location sharing, device telemetry (battery and signal freshness), emergency SOS triggers, and reassurance check-ins.

What is the difference between Piko Hosted and Self-Hosted?

With Piko Hosted, our team manages the cloud server, database, automatic upgrades, and high availability via a subscription. With Self-Hosted Piko, you deploy the software on your own server or home hardware, retaining full control over your data.

Do I need technical expertise to self-host Piko?

Yes, self-hosting requires familiarity with Docker, reverse proxies, domain configuration, and server maintenance. You will also be responsible for backups, system updates, and your own third-party credentials (such as map tiles or push messaging accounts).

Can I migrate between Hosted and Self-Hosted later?

Yes. Piko uses standardized family export formats so you can take your family configuration and telemetry history with you if you decide to switch hosting models.

Does Piko work on Android phones?

Yes, Piko is natively optimized for Android with Material 3 foundation, supporting portrait layouts, battery guardrails, offline breadcrumb storage, and quick tile shortcuts.
