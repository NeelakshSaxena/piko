Got Questions?

Honest Hosting FAQ

Can I start on Piko Hosted and switch to Self-Hosted later?
expand_more
Yes, absolutely. We believe in software freedom. Piko includes full circle export tooling. You can download all your family members, custom safe geofences, and alerts into an encrypted JSON bundle, spin up your Docker container, and import everything in less than 3 minutes.
Do self-hosted instances receive official app updates?
expand_more
Yes. The mobile app published in the Apple App Store and Google Play is the exact same binary. We maintain backwards API compatibility across minor versions. When your family updates their apps from the app stores, your self-hosted instance remains fully compatible.
How do push notifications work when I host my own server?
expand_more
Apple and Google require cryptographically signed developer keys to deliver background wakeups and push notifications. On Piko Hosted, our verified relays handle this. On Self-Hosted, you can plug in your own free Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) credentials, or configure UnifiedPush on Android without any third-party clouds.
What are the minimum hardware specs for Self-Hosting?
expand_more
Piko is written with an ultra-efficient Rust/Go core. A single family circle of 6 devices updating every 15 seconds consumes less than 350MB of RAM and under 2% CPU on a standard Raspberry Pi 4 or an entry-level $5/mo VPS.
